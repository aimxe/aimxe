LOCKBREAK ISLAND - REBUILT

1. Open Roblox Studio.
2. Choose File > Open from File.
3. Select Lockbreak-Island-Rebuilt.rbxlx from this download.
4. Press Play (F5).

No Rojo installation is needed to play this file.

Controls: WASD move, Space jump, E interact, Shift sprint, B shop.
Touch: native movement controls plus buttons on the game HUD.
Gamepad: X interact, L3 sprint; select shop controls with the gamepad.

Steal the keycard in the WEST guard office.
Cut power at the EAST generator.
Optionally rescue inmates and complete side quests.
Escape NORTH at the boat.

Studio starts in practice mode with temporary progress and no Robux purchases.
